Paint Steward prototype

A clickable mock-up of the phone app. It does two jobs: add a paint (scan the can, answer three questions) and look one up (tap a room, see the paint, show it at the store).

Nothing here is real yet. The data is made up, the camera is pretend, and nothing is saved. Best on a phone, or in a narrow desktop window.
